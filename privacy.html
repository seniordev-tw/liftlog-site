Lift Log（重訓日誌）隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 10 月 24 日

你的訓練資料不離開裝置

你輸入的所有資料（動作、課表、訓練紀錄、體重）只儲存在你的裝置上的本機資料庫，不會傳送到我們或任何第三方的伺服器。app 沒有帳號系統；免費版僅使用 Google Firebase Analytics 收集匿名的使用統計（見下方「使用統計」）。

廣告

免費版在畫面底部顯示一條由 Google AdMob 提供的橫幅廣告。為了投放廣告，AdMob SDK 會向 Google 傳送你的裝置廣告 ID、IP 位址推算的概略位置、裝置型號與 app 版本等技術資訊；這些資料由 Google 依其隱私權政策處理，我們自己不會取得或儲存。你可以在裝置的「設定 › Google › 廣告」重設廣告 ID 或選擇退出個人化廣告。

歐洲經濟區、英國與瑞士的使用者在首次啟動時會看到同意選項，之後可在 app 的「設定 › 廣告 › 廣告隱私設定」隨時修改。

訓練中的畫面不顯示廣告。購買「移除廣告」後，app 不再載入廣告 SDK，也不再傳送上述資料。

使用統計

免費版使用 Google Firebase Analytics 收集匿名的使用統計，用來了解廣告與「移除廣告」提示的安排是否讓人流失，並改善 app。會收集：app 的啟動與使用時間、完成訓練的次數、是否出現全螢幕廣告與「移除廣告」提示（及出現的位置）、裝置型號、作業系統與 app 版本、由 IP 位址推算的概略位置，以及 Firebase 為 app 安裝產生的識別碼與裝置廣告 ID。不包含你輸入的動作、重量、次數、筆記或任何訓練內容。這些資料由 Google 依其隱私權政策處理。

歐洲經濟區、英國與瑞士的使用者在同意選項處理完之前不會收集；其他地區在廣告功能初始化後開始收集。購買「移除廣告」後，app 會關閉使用統計；已收集的資料仍保留在 Google 的系統中。你也可以在裝置的「設定 › Google › 廣告」重設廣告 ID。

我們也使用 Firebase Remote Config 做 A/B 測試：隨機決定部分使用者是否會看到全螢幕廣告，用來評估廣告對使用體驗的影響。分組依 Firebase 為 app 安裝產生的識別碼，不涉及你的姓名或訓練內容；與使用統計一樣，要在同意選項處理完之後才啟用，購買「移除廣告」後不再抓取。

應用程式內購買

「移除廣告」是透過 Google Play 完成的一次性購買。付款資料由 Google Play 處理，我們不會取得你的付款資訊；購買紀錄綁定你的 Google 帳號，換裝置後可在「設定 › 廣告 › 還原購買」還原。退款依 Google Play 的退款政策辦理。

權限用途

  • 通知、鬧鐘與提醒：只用來在組間休息結束時提醒你，不會發送其他通知。
  • 震動：休息結束時提示。
  • 檔案存取／分享：只在你主動「匯出備份」或「匯入備份」時，讀寫你選擇的 JSON 檔。

備份

你可以隨時把全部資料匯出成 JSON 檔並自行保管；匯出檔的儲存與分享由你的裝置與你選擇的服務處理。解除安裝 app 會刪除裝置上的所有資料。

兒童

Lift Log Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Your training data stays on your device

Everything you enter (exercises, routines, workouts, body weight) is stored only in a local database on your device and is never sent to us or to any third party. There are no accounts; the free version uses only Google Firebase Analytics for anonymous usage statistics (see “Usage statistics” below).

Advertising

The free version shows one banner ad at the bottom of the screen, served by Google AdMob. To serve ads, the AdMob SDK sends Google your device's advertising ID, an approximate location derived from your IP address, and technical details such as device model and app version. Google processes this data under its Privacy Policy; we never receive or store it. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device's Settings › Google › Ads.

Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent on first launch and can change their choice at any time in the app under Settings › Ads › Ad privacy options.

No ads are shown while a workout is in progress. After purchasing "Remove ads" the app no longer loads the ad SDK and no longer sends the data above.

Usage statistics

The free version uses Google Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage statistics, to understand whether the placement of ads and “remove ads” prompts makes people stop using the app, and to improve it. It collects: app launches and usage time, the number of workouts you finish, whether a full-screen ad or a “remove ads” prompt was shown (and where), device model, operating system and app version, an approximate location derived from your IP address, and the identifier Firebase generates for each installation together with the device advertising ID. It does not include the exercises, weights, reps, notes or any other workout content you enter. Google processes this data under its Privacy Policy.

For users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, nothing is collected until the consent choice has been handled; elsewhere collection starts once the ad feature initialises. After you purchase “Remove ads” the app turns usage statistics off; data already collected remains in Google's systems. You can also reset your advertising ID under your device's Settings › Google › Ads.

We also use Firebase Remote Config for A/B tests: it randomly decides whether some users see full-screen ads, so we can evaluate the effect of ads on the experience. Grouping is based on the identifier Firebase generates for each installation, not on your name or workout content; like usage statistics it is enabled only after the consent choice has been handled, and it is no longer fetched after you purchase “Remove ads”.

In-app purchase

"Remove ads" is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Payment details are handled by Google Play and never reach us. The purchase is tied to your Google account and can be restored on a new device under Settings › Ads › Restore purchase. Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy.

Permissions

  • Notifications, alarms & reminders: used only to notify you when a rest period ends.
  • Vibration: used for the rest-over cue.
  • File access / sharing: used only when you explicitly export or import a backup JSON file you choose.

Backups

You can export all your data to a JSON file at any time. Storing and sharing that file is handled by your device and the services you choose. Uninstalling the app deletes all data on the device.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
